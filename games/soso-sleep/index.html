> WHAT IS IT_ > 這是什麼_

SoSoSheep helps you drift off by tapping sheep over a fence with serene soundscapes. Smart Auto-Standby dims the session if you miss enough times — so bedtime can end as gently as it began. SoSoSheep 用輕點綿羊跳過圍欄，搭配寧靜聲景幫助入睡。Smart Auto-Standby 在連續失誤後自動變暗待機，讓睡前結束得同樣溫柔。

> FEATURES_ > 重點功能_

  • Sheep counting with soft motion 柔和動畫的數羊互動
  • Sleep music that can slow as you settle 可隨計數逐漸放慢的助眠音樂
  • Night Companions board & goodnight hugs 夜間同伴板與道晚安抱抱
  • 10-day sleep journal as a gentle star map 十日睡眠日記（溫柔星圖）
