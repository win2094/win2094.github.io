> WHAT IS IT_ > 這是什麼_

Happy Polygon is a free neon arcade suite for iPhone by independent designer LAP WAI HSU (HSU Tsui). Bold geometric visuals, quick sessions, local high scores, and Apple Game Center leaderboards. Happy Polygon 是獨立設計師 LAP WAI HSU（許萃）為 iPhone 打造的免費霓虹街機合集。幾何視覺、短局爽感、本地高分與 Apple Game Center 排行榜。
